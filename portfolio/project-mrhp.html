Project Overview

Micro Reactor Heat Pipe (MRHP) is a novel conceptual reactor with heat pipe as a coolant. MRHP previously uses Uranium dioxide (UO2) as a fuel. A new fuel, Uranium Carbide (UC), was used as MRHP's fuel in this experiment. With this new fuel, a few key performance parameters of the nuclear reactor were observed which are the correct enrichment level for MRHP with UC, the effect of temperature changes to reactivity, and the power distribution in MRHP core. An open-source Monte Carlo code OpenMC was used in this analysis. This study resulted in a lower fuel enrichment of UC valued at 11.1 %, a good negative coefficient of temperature reactivity at -2.0084 pcm/K, and 2.07 of the total power peaking factor.

This work is my Nuclear Engineering Bachelor’s Thesis titled "Analysis of the Temperature Reactivity Coefficient and the Power Peaking Factor of Micro Reactor Heat Pipe (MRHP) with Uranium Carbide Using OpenMC" at Universitas Gadjah Mada.
